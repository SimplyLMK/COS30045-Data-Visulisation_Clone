# Power Electronics – Data Story Visualisation

## Data Story
This visualisation explores **energy consumption trends in televisions** sold in the Australian market.  

### Audience
The primary audience is **Australian households and consumers** who are considering purchasing a new television.  
With rising electricity prices, energy efficiency is becoming a key decision factor when buying appliances.  

### Why this matters
- **Consumers** benefit by identifying the most efficient screen technologies and sizes to save on energy bills.  
- **Policy makers** gain insights into market adoption of energy-efficient technologies.  
- **Manufacturers** can better understand consumer demand for efficiency and large-screen formats.  

The story highlights that **LCD/LED dominate the market**, plasma is declining, and larger screens generally consume more power but can still achieve high star ratings thanks to technological improvements.

---

## About the Data
- **Source:** [Australian Government Energy Rating Register](https://www.energyrating.gov.au/) (downloaded September 2025).  
- **Processing:**  
  - Filtered entries for the Australian market.  
  - Removed duplicates.  
  - Converted screen dimensions from cm to inches.  
  - Grouped televisions into screen size categories.  
- **Privacy:** Dataset contains no personal or sensitive information; only appliance specifications.  
- **Accuracy and Limitations:**  
  - Data relies on manufacturer submissions, which may contain discrepancies.  
  - Some models may not be up to date or fully representative of the current retail market.  
- **Ethics:**  
  - Analysis is provided to promote **informed consumer choice** and **sustainability**.  
  - No manipulation of results was done to favor any manufacturer or product.
